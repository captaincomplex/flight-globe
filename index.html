Flight Globes

Interactive 3D globes of the flights in flights.json, after James Harding's flight globes.

  • Every FlightAll routes at once, with animated traffic along each arc.
  • SequentialFlights light up one at a time, in date order.
  • CountriesCountries visited, raised by how often they were flown to.
  • Destinations HeatmapBars (or a heat gradient) at each destination, sized by visit count.
  • TimelineReplay the flights in order with play/pause and a scrubber.
